# Facts Extracted from the SIVEP Technical Specifications Document

Source: `assets/SIVEP_Cahier_de_charge_du_programme_des_logements.pdf` (44 pages, "Cahier des Prescriptions Techniques Particulières" — CPTP, signed by Directeur Général Amadou Dieng).

**Important framing:** this is a **construction technical-specifications document** (the technical clauses a contractor bids against — materials, methods, standards for each trade), not an architectural design book with site plans/renderings, not a bill of quantities with pricing, and not a financial model. It answers "what will it be built with and to what standard," not "how many units, on what land, for how much." Sections 3, 8, and 11-13 of the proposal outline still need those inputs from elsewhere.

## Confirmed facts

- **Developer/Sponsor:** SIVEP — Société d'Investissement des Villes et Pôles. Signed by **Amadou Dieng, Directeur Général**.
- **Location:** "les logements Dakar et les régions" — the program spans **Dakar and other regions**, i.e. this is a **multi-site national housing program**, not a single development on one plot. This changes how §3/§7 (site-specific land tenure) should be framed — land tenure risk may need to be assessed per site, not once.
- **Unit types offered:**
  | Type | Rooms | Sizes |
  |---|---|---|
  | **F3** | Living room + 1 master bedroom + 1 bedroom + kitchen + toilet + family space | Salon 15m², Master bedroom 12m², Bedroom 10m², Kitchen 6m², Toilet 4m², Family space 3.65m² |
  | **F4** | Living room + 2 bedrooms + kitchen + toilet + bathroom + family space | Salon 15.48m², Master bedroom 14.36m², Bathroom 3.41m², Bedroom 10m², Bedroom 10m², Kitchen 6m², Toilet 4m², Family space 13.16m² |
- **Construction system:** conventional reinforced concrete frame (BAEL 91 rules), FE500/FE400 rebar, hollow-block masonry walls (0.20m/0.10m), **hourdis creux (hollow-core slab) floor system**, monocouche self-protected waterproofing on roof terraces, ceramic tile flooring (grès cérame) + faience wall tile in wet rooms, red-wood ("bois rouge") interior doors with mandatory anti-termite treatment, aluminum window/door joinery (alloy 6060, anodized), standard PVC water supply/drainage, NFC 15-100 compliant electrical wiring with per-room fixture schedules, standard interior/exterior paint (Pantex 1200 or equivalent).
- **Septic/sanitation:** on-site sanitation via septic tank ("fosse étanche, 1 per 10 users") + soakaway pit — **implies the housing program is NOT assumed to be on a centralized municipal sewer network**, consistent with a peri-urban/greenfield site.
- **Electrical supply assumption:** "Le bâtiment sera alimenté à partir du réseau existant à proximité" (building will be supplied from the existing nearby grid) — **standard grid connection only; zero mention of solar/PV/renewable energy anywhere in this 44-page document.** This confirms the PV component is a genuinely new addition the Chinese partner would bring to the project, not something already designed in — consistent with our research finding that Chinese solar involvement in Senegal has been an equity/EPC opportunity area, not yet integrated into local developers' base building specs.
- **Standards referenced:** French DTU technical standards throughout (DTU 43, 60.1, 60.33, 37.1, 39.1/39.4, etc.), NF (French) product/testing standards, cross-referenced against "normes en vigueur au Sénégal" (standards in force in Senegal) — i.e. Senegalese construction practice runs on French normative standards, relevant for the Chinese EPC/contractor's technical interface if they're unfamiliar with DTU-based specification (vs. Chinese GB standards).
- **Quality control:** mandatory third-party "Bureau de Contrôle" (technical control office) oversight throughout construction, weekly site-meeting attendance, decennial insurance (garantie décennale) required of the contractor — standard, but should be priced into the JV's cost/insurance assumptions.

## What is still missing (needed to complete §1, §3, §4, §8, §11-13 of the proposal)

1. **Total unit count and site-by-site breakdown** — how many F3 vs F4 units, at how many sites, what's the phasing.
2. **Total site area(s) / GFA** — no hectare or m² total given.
3. **Land tenure status per site** — critical for §6/§7 given the multi-site, "Dakar and regions" framing; different sites could sit in different tenure categories (domaine national vs. État vs. titre foncier).
4. **Costs/pricing** — this is a technical spec, not a priced bill of quantities; no construction cost basis is available from this document.
5. **Site plans, renderings, exact addresses/coordinates** — expected from the video and/or a separate design/site-plan document.
6. **Sales/rental pricing and target buyer profile** — needed for §4 (market) and §8 (financial structure).
7. **Current project status** — is this an active program already under construction, or a program being scoped for this investment round? The document reads as a standing technical-specs template SIVEP uses across its housing program, which is useful context but doesn't establish which specific phase/site this investment proposal covers.

**Recommended next question to the client:** which specific site(s)/phase of the SIVEP program is this investment round actually financing — is it the whole multi-region program or one identified site? That answer determines whether §7's land-tenure risk assessment needs to be done once or multiple times.
