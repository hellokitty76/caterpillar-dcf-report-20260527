## Deal shape confirmed with client (2026-07-07)
- **Rental model:** 2,000 villas are build-to-rent, single-owner, leased to Chantifa cooperative members → income-yield asset, not build-to-sell.
- **Chinese side = equity investor**, negotiating via the **Shanghai Chamber of Commerce (上海商会)**.
- **Strategic angle = market entry:** the project is a beachhead for Chinese building-materials/equipment exports into West Africa (the tech specs already require imported steel, aluminum, tiles, sanitaryware, electrical gear + solar panels). Sinosure/Eximbank export financing is a natural fit.
- **Land:** 60 ha reported **already allocated (划拨)** — exact legal instrument still to be confirmed.
- **Name:** confirmed **SIVEP** (video's "CIVEP" was a transcription slip).
